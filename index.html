A clear process

Know the steps before you commit.

A responsible loan process should explain what information is needed, how decisions are made and what the loan will cost.

  • Submit your contact details and the amount you want to request.
  • Discuss eligibility and provide any required documents through a verified channel.
  • Review a written offer with the interest rate, fees, total repayment and due dates.
  • Accept only when you understand the agreement and can afford the instalments.

Frequently asked questions

Important things to know

Will I automatically receive UGX 12,000,500?

No. That is the maximum amount a visitor can request through this form, not a promise, approval or guaranteed lending limit. Any real offer depends on the lender's eligibility assessment and terms.

Is the 10,000 UGX fee required?

This website does not confirm or collect that fee. Before paying any upfront charge, independently verify the business, the reason for the charge, its refund policy and the official payment recipient. An upfront fee does not guarantee approval or disbursement.

What are the interest rate and repayment amount?

They have not been configured because no verified rate or fee schedule was supplied. Request a written disclosure of the annual or periodic interest rate, all fees, total amount repayable, instalment schedule and late-payment consequences before accepting a loan.

Can I pay with MTN or Airtel Money?

The page is designed to support information for both methods, but official merchant details must be added and verified by the business. Do not send money to a number solely because it appears in an unverified message.

Does the form send my message automatically?

No. It opens WhatsApp with a draft message. You can review it and choose whether to send it. This static HTML page does not store applications in a database or verify loan eligibility.

What should I check before borrowing?

Verify the lender's identity and authorisation with the relevant Ugandan regulator, read the full agreement, compare the total cost, check the repayment dates and make sure instalments fit your budget. Never share your mobile-money PIN or OTP.
